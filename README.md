"Front Row" is a single quiet moment of earned pride. No grand speech, no trophy — just a mother who spent eleven years working night shifts finally watching her daughter graduate, and allowing herself, for once, to sit where she belongs: in the front row.

## The line

> After eleven years of night shifts, she finally watched her daughter graduate, and for once she let herself sit in the front row.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) holds the line as a constant, checks that it is a single line of 1–240 characters, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, then re-reads the file and verifies it matches exactly. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
